description: "Download the TT Arena Player app and sign in with just your phone number, no password required."

The TT Arena Player app is free, and it's how you follow your own draw, schedule, and results once you're registered for a tournament.

## Install the app

Download **TT Arena Players** from the Google Play Store. An iOS version is on the way.

## Log in with your phone number

TT Arena doesn't use passwords. Open the app and enter the same phone number you registered with.

<Frame caption="Signing in with a phone number and a 6-digit code.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/player-app-login-otp.png" alt="TT Arena Player app phone number and OTP entry screen" />
</Frame>

You'll get a 6-digit code by SMS. On most Android phones the app picks this up automatically the moment the text arrives, so you often won't even need to open your messages, just wait a second or two and you'll be signed in. If it doesn't auto-fill, type the code in yourself.

## You're in

Once you're signed in, any tournament you've registered for with that phone number shows up automatically.
