Once you're signed in, your Home tab lists every tournament you're registered for. Open one to get to its Draw, Schedule, and Profile tabs, which cover everything you need during the event.

<Frame caption="Home, with an upcoming tournament.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/player-app-home.png" alt="TT Arena Player app Home tab showing a registered tournament" />
</Frame>

## Draw

See the full bracket for your category, exactly as the organizer has it set up, group standings if the tournament has a group stage, and the knockout bracket once it's generated.

## Schedule

This is the tab you'll check most often during an event. It has two views:

- **Your Schedule** shows just your own matches: anything live right now, what's coming up next, and what you've already completed.
- **Tournament Schedule** shows the organizer's full day-by-day schedule across every table.

<Frame caption="Your Schedule, right after a win.">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/player-app-draw-schedule.png" alt="TT Arena Player app Schedule tab with a Won match card" />
</Frame>

The moment a referee records your result, it shows up here, along with which table and umpire for your next match once it's assigned. You'll also get a notification so you don't have to keep the app open and watch it.

## Profile

Keep your display name, photo, academy or club, city, and equipment details up to date. This is also where you'll find notification settings and a quick way to rate the app if you're enjoying it.
