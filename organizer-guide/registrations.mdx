The Registrations tab is where every player who has registered, or been added, shows up.

## Reviewing a registration

Each entry shows the player's full name, date of birth, gender, city, district, academy or club, and the category they've entered. Open any entry to see the complete details before deciding.

## Approving a registration

Select **Approve** on an entry once you've checked it over. Approved players move into the draw once you generate it for their category.

## Editing a registration

If a player made a mistake, or you need to correct something on their behalf, select **Edit** on their entry and update the details directly.

## Adding a player manually

Not every player registers themselves ahead of time. Select **Add registration** to enter a player's details yourself, useful for walk-ins or last-minute entries on the day.
