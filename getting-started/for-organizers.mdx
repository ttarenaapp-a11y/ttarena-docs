title: Creating your first tournament
description: "How to sign in to TT Arena, create a tournament, and get your registration link ready to share with players."
---

This walks through getting a tournament set up for the first time. It takes a few minutes.

## 1. Sign in

Go to [play.ttarena.app](https://play.ttarena.app) and sign in with your organizer account. If this is your first time, reach out to the TT Arena team to get an account set up.

## 2. Create the tournament

From your dashboard, select **New tournament** and fill in the basics:

- **Name**, what players will see, e.g. "Sample Open 2026"
- **City**
- **Start date** and **End date**

Once you save, the tournament shows up under **Your tournaments** and opens straight into its settings.

## 3. Fill in the rest of the settings

From here you'll want to set your categories (Singles, Doubles, or both), how many tables you have available, and a registration deadline. See [Tournament settings](/organizer-guide/tournament-settings) for the full walkthrough.

## 4. Share the registration link

Every tournament gets its own public registration page at `play.ttarena.app/register/<tournament-id>`. Share that link with your players, by WhatsApp, on a poster, wherever you'd normally advertise the event. No account is needed on their end to register, a login only comes in later if they want to track their matches from the Player app.
